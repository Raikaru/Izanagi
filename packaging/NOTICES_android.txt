Third-party software in the Android package
==========================================
No Persona 4 disc data is included. Supply your own USA PS2 disc image.

SDL 3.2.10: https://libsdl.org, zlib licence (licenses/SDL3-zlib.txt).
LodePNG 20261006: zlib licence (licenses/LodePNG-zlib.txt).
cgltf 1.15 (Johannes Kuhlmann): MIT licence (licenses/cgltf-MIT.txt).
FFmpeg 7.1.1: https://ffmpeg.org, LGPL 2.1 or later
(licenses/FFmpeg-LGPL-2.1.txt). Only MPEG-2 video, ADX audio, avutil and
swscale are built. No GPL or nonfree components are enabled.

Complete unmodified upstream source archives and the exact build recipe are in
assets/source inside this APK/AAB. FFmpeg and SDL are dynamically linked,
separate lib/<abi>/*.so files. You may replace these with ABI-compatible builds:
unzip the APK, replace the libraries, zipalign, and sign with your own key using
Android SDK apksigner. Android requires every APK to be signed; the developer's
private signing key is not required to install your modified APK. Back up saves
before changing signing identities (Android refuses an in-place update signed
by another key). Reverse engineering for debugging modifications to the LGPL
libraries is permitted.

Luau 0.744 (when enabled): MIT, including inherited Lua notices
(licenses/Luau-MIT.txt and licenses/Lua-MIT.txt). The exact vendored source and
local patches are in assets/source/luau-source.tar.gz (aapt may remove the outer
gzip wrapper).

Android NDK libc++ / libc++abi, unwind and compiler-rt (when the C++ adapter is
linked) are static. Their Apache 2.0 / LLVM-exception and component notices are
in licenses/LLVM-libcxx.txt and the Android-NDK notice files. The actual NDK
version, source revision, archive hashes and source location are in
assets/source/runtime-provenance.txt.

dlmalloc: public domain; miniz 3.0.2: MIT (see source third_party/miniz/LICENSE).
Okumura LZARI and Ross Ridge mymc, adapted in the MAX save importer: public domain.
newlib stdio/stdlib (UC Berkeley, AT&T), adapted in the translated EE stdio cores: BSD (licenses/newlib-BSD.txt).
CRI middleware headers from recvx-decomp (AshfordFamily): MIT (licenses/recvx-decomp-MIT.txt).
Parts of the renderer follow sky2hook by tge-was-taken, used with permission;
see third_party/README.md in the port source.
